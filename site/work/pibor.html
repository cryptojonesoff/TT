Digital Management & Marketing

Pibor

A self-initiated 3D product animation, built to show a Swiss precision-manufacturing brand what's possible for their own marketing.

CONTEXT

Pibor manufactures precision components for the watchmaking industry in Switzerland. As part of a wider look at regional brands whose digital presence has room to grow, their work stood out as strong material for a kind of 3D storytelling most manufacturers in their space aren't using yet.

INTERVENTION

We built a speculative 3D animation of a watch-crown assembly, exploding it into its nine components and calling out materials, finishes and certifications one by one. The parts are generic reference models, not Pibor's own technical drawings, made to demonstrate the format itself: a version built from their real components and CAD files would go further still. Animations like this serve a brand beyond social feeds and websites too, at trade shows, where manufacturers meet clients, prospects and partners face to face.

RESULT

Presented directly to Pibor, who responded with real interest. Not a delivered project, but a concrete look at what's possible, and the start of a conversation.
