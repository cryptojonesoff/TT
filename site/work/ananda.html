Brand & Digital Design

Ananda Bungalows

Market study, brand identity and an immersive, photography-led website, built end to end for a boutique bungalow stay in General Luna, Siargao.

Ananda relies entirely on Airbnb and Booking.com for reservations. No site was going to change that. What it needed was a standalone brand presence that builds enough trust before a guest ever reaches the listing. We ran the audience and positioning research, built the identity, and designed and developed the full site: three pieces of one engagement, covered below.

01. Market study / 02. Branding & identity / 03. Website creation
01

Market study.

Understanding who reaches this listing, before designing anything to show them.

Three audiences overlap on Ananda's listing: couples seeking a calm, romantic stay (primary), surfers drawn to Siargao's breaks, and long-stay digital nomads, all arriving pre-sold from Instagram, Google or an Airbnb/Booking search, often mid-comparison across several Siargao properties on their phone. None of them are here to fill out a form or pick dates on a hotel site; they're deciding, in seconds, whether the place feels right.

That reframed the brief. This isn't a booking engine competing with the OTA listing. It's a trust-building layer the listing can't provide on its own: a single-objective site whose only job is to build enough desire that the click-through happens. Every section was tested against one question: does it move a visitor closer to "Book on Airbnb," or is it decoration.

The category

Generic OTA templates and tropical-resort microsites: carousel galleries, star-rating badges, dense amenity grids, all built to keep the guest inside someone else's booking flow.

The gap

Nothing building trust before the OTA click: no standalone identity for a guest already comparing several Siargao stays in the same browser tab.

The positioning

A quiet, boutique-hotel brand site pacing toward one click, resolved into one line: "Arrive somewhere someone already loves."

TEMPLATES RULED OUT ON PURPOSE

The OTA mirror

Star badges and blue-link CTAs would echo the Airbnb tab the visitor is already in, giving no extra reason to trust it.

The resort cliché

Turquoise gradients and "paradise awaits" copy read as stock, not as a real place a couple can picture staying in.

The SaaS landing page

Hero-metric blocks and uppercase eyebrows are borrowed startup grammar that undercuts the quiet boutique-hotel register the brand needed.

Left: the internal positioning deck (audience segments, target markets, material direction), excluded from the guest-facing site on purpose because it's a strategy artifact, not guest photography. Right: the reef-break photograph that opens the live site.

02

Branding & identity.

Golden-hour jungle bungalow: surf culture and boutique calm carried in one mark.

The crest pairs a surfboard silhouette with a palm frond and a lantern, translating "Ananda" (bliss, in Sanskrit) into a mark that reads as both island and retreat without leaning on a single tourist cliché. The brand had to hold two things at once: Siargao's surf-town energy, and a calm, premium register, confident and understated rather than persuasive, closer to a quiet boutique hotel than a tourist brochure.

Off-white and palm green dominate as the primary light/dark surfaces; sand is the neutral secondary; terracotta is a supporting accent; coral is reserved for exactly one thing: the "Book on Airbnb" CTA. Every pairing was checked against relative-luminance contrast rather than eyeballed: the coral CTA needed dark ink text rather than white to clear 4.5:1, and the terracotta kicker was darkened twice to hold AA on both off-white and sand.

03

Website creation.

Design, structure, implementation and features: one continuous scroll built to earn a single click.

DESIGN

The homepage opens on a single full-bleed photo, flanked by the words "Ananda" and "Bungalows," that grows to fill the screen as the visitor scrolls, drags or presses a key. Not a video, a scroll-driven expansion of a real photograph. From there the page runs as one continuous scroll: no cards, no boxed panels, no persistent nav. The ten property photos are the argument; the interface's only job is to never compete with them.

Two of the ten property photographs carrying the site. Chrome stays quiet enough to never compete with them.

STRUCTURE

One page, six moments, no persistent nav: an expanding hero, a quiet intro, a full-bleed arrival photo, the gallery, a full-bleed island video, and a closing call to action, each behaving differently rather than reusing one scroll effect throughout. The only repeating chrome is a floating "Book" bar that appears once the hero has passed and steps aside again as the footer comes into view.

IMPLEMENTATION
  • ✓

    Scroll-hijacked expanding hero, no library

    Wheel, touch and keyboard input all map to a single 0–1 progress value that scales a photo box from a collapsed rectangle, sized to the gap between the "Ananda" / "Bungalows" wordmark, up to full-bleed, crossfading the background and scrim as it grows, then releases scroll-jacking entirely once expanded.

  • ✓

    IntersectionObserver reveal system

    Photos crossfade only, text blocks fade and rise, small marks scale in: three distinct reveal behaviors, not one uniform effect, all bypassed instantly under prefers-reduced-motion.

  • ✓

    Viewport-gated video

    The full-bleed island video only loads and plays while its section is on screen. An IntersectionObserver toggles play()/pause() and defers the initial load() until the visitor scrolls there.

  • ✓

    Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, no framework

    One dependency-free script drives the hero, the reveal system, the parallax and the floating bar; rAF-throttled scroll handlers skip any element currently off-screen.

Two of the full-bleed parallax photos. Factor tuned per section (0.16 here, 0.18 on the closing CTA) rather than one global value.

FEATURES

A quiet floating "Book" bar is the only repeating chrome on the page. It appears once the hero has scrolled past and steps aside again as the footer's own CTA comes into view, so the ask stays present without nagging. A full-bleed aerial video widens the pitch from this room to this island at the exact point desire peaks.

Two real CTAs appear everywhere a CTA appears: "Book on Airbnb" filled in coral, "Book on Booking.com" outlined in terracotta, equal visual weight, no false urgency copy, both real anchor links straight to the listings. No fake date pickers, no fabricated availability or reviews. Only facts sourced from the client or the photos.
