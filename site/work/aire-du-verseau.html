Brand & Digital Design

L'Aire du Verseau

CONTEXT

A 1700 Jura farmhouse fully renovated in 2024, needing a website that could speak convincingly to two very different audiences — corporate seminar bookers and families — without feeling like two separate businesses.

INTERVENTION

We built a site structured around dual use-cases, with an integrated booking-request flow, room and space galleries, and a video tour of the main living space.

RESULT

A single site that lets one property serve both buyer types directly, generating booking inquiries without a middleman.
