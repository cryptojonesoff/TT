Personal brand & content platform

Jonas Mouttet

CONTEXT

A personal brand platform to unify years of personal-development content — training, nutrition, mindset and finance — under one coherent identity, rather than scattered across social channels.

We designed and built a content platform structured around three core pillars (Body / Mind / Finance), each with its own landing video, article library, and an integrated shop.

RESULT

A live personal brand platform giving years of content a single, navigable home across video, articles and commerce.
