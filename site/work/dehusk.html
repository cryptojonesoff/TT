Brand & Digital Design

Dehusk

CONTEXT

Dehusk is launching the Philippines' first locally-produced barista coconut milk — a direct-to-consumer brand needing an e-commerce site that could carry a premium, dairy-alternative positioning.

INTERVENTION

We designed and built the full brand site — product storytelling, nutrition breakdown, flavour lineup, and a direct shop flow — pairing bold typography with tropical, ingredient-led photography.

RESULT

A live, shop-ready brand site supporting Dehusk's product launch and retail storytelling.
